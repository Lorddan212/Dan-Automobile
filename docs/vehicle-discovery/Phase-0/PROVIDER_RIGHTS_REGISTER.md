## 2. Additional sources and explicit exclusions

- [Optional enrichment review](OPEN_DATA_ENRICHMENT_REVIEW.md) records EPA Test Car List/Trends (conditional schema/rights/join review), manufacturer publications (free reading, **not open reuse permission**), CarAPI's limited free dataset (plan/storage/redistribution restrictions), and Unsplash (conditional editorial imagery).
- EPA default open-data policy is evidence for EPA-produced test datasets with metadata review; it does **not resolve** the separately administered FuelEconomy.gov feed terms.
- Free trials, sandbox credits, API Ninjas noncommercial/evaluation access and paid image products are **unsuitable as mandatory free v1 dependencies**.
- No provider email sent; no license has been obtained by assumption. Drafts: [PROVIDER_INQUIRY_DRAFTS.md](PROVIDER_INQUIRY_DRAFTS.md).

## 3. Register maintenance and closure

Before enabling a provider record its official policy URL, version/date, scope of permission (display/cache/storage/normalization/redistribution), attribution, automation budget, retention/deletion conditions and reviewer. Preserve written permission reference with access controls; do not commit secrets or personal correspondence indiscriminately. On expiry/restriction/conflict stop new ingestion/publication and use a permitted last-valid fallback or unpublish affected enrichment. Rights and identity gates are independent.

**Open:** EPA clarification; vPIC redistribution clarification; owner selection of [eligible hosting](HOSTING_ASSESSMENT.md); final image admission and fixture rights. Optional sources cannot weaken these gates.
